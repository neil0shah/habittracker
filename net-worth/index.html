Accounts

Each statement you upload is matched to an account by institution and account number — a new one is created automatically the first time. Every extracted balance and date is editable right here, so a misread PDF is a quick fix, not a bad data point. A transfer between two of your own accounts (e.g. Bank of America → Robinhood) is never double-counted: net worth is the sum of each account's own latest balance, so one account's drop is simply offset by the other's rise.
